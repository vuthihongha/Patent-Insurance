Patent risk

Risk at every stage

  1. 01

    Before grant

    The application is refused and costs are sunk.

  2. 02

    After grant

    The patent may face cancellation or invalidation.

  3. 03

    During use

    Sued for infringement, or infringed by others.

  4. 04

    After expiry

    Legal risk can persist after the right ends.

Why are patents vulnerable?

  • 01

    A negative right

    A patent lets you exclude others; it does not guarantee your own freedom to operate.

  • 02

    Can be invalidated

    Even after examination, a patent can be revoked at any time.

  • 03

    Law meets technology

    Scope depends on how the claims are interpreted.

Features

How is it different?

  • Non-life insurance

    Created by contract, works like civil insurance.

  • Intangible subject

    Intellectual assets are hard to quantify and price.

  • Focused on infringement

    Both defending claims and enforcing your rights.

  • Financial support

    Pays the cost of resolving disputes instead of restoring an asset, as traditional insurance does.

Common doubts

Is patent insurance feasible?

01Is patent risk too uncertain to insure?

Risk arises at well-defined procedural points and falls into five groups: intrinsic, environmental, innovation, licensing demands and NPEs. It can be identified and managed.

02Is it impossible to price?

Insurers can use proxies such as dispute frequency, duration and average awards. Risk can also be split into layers and spread through reinsurance.

03Too expensive for SMEs?

The State can subsidise early premiums, as Japan and China do. Tiered products let SMEs buy smaller cover. Premiums fall as market data builds up.
