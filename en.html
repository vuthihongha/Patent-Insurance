FAQ

What businesses ask

01Can I buy patent insurance in Vietnam today?

Not yet. There is no dedicated patent insurance product on the market and no specific legal provisions. The research proposes a roadmap to build this market.

02Can small businesses take part?

Yes. The research proposes screening by technological capability and innovation activity, not by size. Decree 268/2025/ND-CP widened S&T enterprise status to include innovation criteria.

03Does insurance pay out for wilful infringement?

No. Wilful infringement and fraud are excluded, so insurance does not weaken the deterrent effect of IP law.

04How much would it cost?

Vietnam has no premium schedule yet. In the United States, premiums are typically 1–5% of the insured value. The research proposes partial State premium support during the pilot.

05When should a business get insured?

Application insurance fits from the time of filing. For the other products, the research suggests insuring at grant, before any specific litigation risk arises.

06Is patent insurance mandatory?

No. The research proposes a voluntary scheme with State support, considering semi-mandatory cover for some high-risk sectors only once the market matures.
