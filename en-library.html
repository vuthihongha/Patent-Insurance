Glossary

Patent insurance dictionary

Patent insuranceBảo hiểm sáng chế
A mechanism for transferring and spreading patent risk.
Patent riskRủi ro sáng chế
Risk arising when obtaining, maintaining, enforcing and exploiting patent rights.
Patent application insuranceBảo hiểm cấp phép
Covers filing costs if the application is not granted.
Enforcement insuranceBảo hiểm thực thi quyền
Pays the cost of suing infringers of your patent.
Defence insuranceBảo hiểm phòng vệ
Pays defence costs when you are accused of infringement.
Infringement liability insuranceBảo hiểm trách nhiệm xâm phạm
Pays costs and damages for unintentional infringement.
Legal expenses insurance (LEI)Bảo hiểm chi phí pháp lý
Funds attorneys and litigation in disputes.
Co-insuranceĐồng bảo hiểm
The insured bears a share of each loss.
ReinsuranceTái bảo hiểm
An insurer passes part of its risk to another party.
DeductibleMức khấu trừ
The part of a loss the insured pays first.
Freedom to operate (FTO)Tự do khai thác
A review confirming a product does not infringe others' patents.
Non-practising entity (NPE)Chủ thể không thực hành sáng chế
A patent holder that mainly litigates rather than produces.
LicenceLi-xăng
A contract allowing another party to use a patent.
CollateralTài sản bảo đảm
Assets, including patent rights, pledged to secure a loan.
CommercialisationThương mại hóa
Putting a patent into production and business to create value.
Innovation ecosystemHệ sinh thái đổi mới sáng tạo
A network of actors that create, protect and use knowledge.
IVS 210Chuẩn mực thẩm định giá quốc tế 210
The international standard for valuing intangible assets.
